# Cable Box Requirements

- Clear interior space, including the closed lid: 7 x 7 x 5 in (177.8 x 177.8 x 127 mm).
- Add a retaining frame for a nominal 4 x 4 in block with 0.4 mm clearance per side. Shift the frame 17.35 mm right and 17.35 mm toward the front.
- Add two 1/2 in-wide, 40 mm-deep U-notches on each wall without the large opening: back, left, and right.
- Add one beveled 1 in-wide vertical slot on the front wall, offset 1 in left of the retainer center. Shift the slot right by the same amount as the retainer and preserve the 1 in relative offset. The slot spans 78% of the interior height and opens through the top edge.
- Apply 0.8 mm bevels to the box, lid, retainer, cavity transitions, and cable-opening rims.
- Use a removable snap-fit lid with a pull lip on one side.
- Design for PLA FDM printing; fit clearances remain adjustable for printer calibration.
